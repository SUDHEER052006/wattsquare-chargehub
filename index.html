The technology

Built a generation ahead
of the chargers on the road.

Most charging points in India are a box on a wall running fixed power. We are building dynamic, intelligent, fully monitored hubs — the kind of infrastructure a national EV fleet actually runs on.

Dynamic power sharing

The site's full capacity is pooled, not split evenly. Each vehicle draws the fastest rate it can safely accept, and the spare power moves to whoever needs it — so your charge doesn't halve when the next car arrives.

Live uptime monitoring

Every gun reports its own health to the CPMS continuously. A fault takes the unit off the map before anyone drives to it — which is the single biggest reason people stop trusting a charging network.

AI route planning

Give it a start, a destination and your current charge. It places the stops, estimates arrival, and routes you around whatever is busy — across our hubs and 10,000+ partner chargers through Statiq.

One-tap payments

Scan the QR and pay by UPI, card or RFID. No app download, no membership, no card machine to hunt for. The price is on the screen before a single unit flows.

Fleet-grade dashboard

Per-vehicle and per-driver consumption, cost centres, session history and exportable reports — with monthly credit billing on a single invoice instead of per-session payments.

Every connector, every vehicle

CCS2, Bharat DC-001 and Type 2 AC on the same forecourt, with bay geometry and cable reach planned for intercity coaches and trucks — not just hatchbacks.

How it works

Three steps. No app required.

You don't need an account, a card reader or a membership. Pull in, plug in, pay with the phone in your pocket.

STEP 01

Find the nearest site

Open the live map to see which guns are free right now, what they cost, and how far off your route the stop is.

STEP 02

Plug in and tap to pay

Scan the QR on the dispenser and pay by UPI, debit card or RFID tag. Charging starts the moment payment clears.

STEP 03

Drive off in ~20 minutes

Site power is shared intelligently across the guns in use, so you get the fastest safe rate your car can accept. Pay only for the units you take.
